PRIVACY

Privacy

This page describes the intended privacy approach for the current Toolivo site. It should be updated if analytics, advertising, forms, accounts, or other third-party services are added.

Browser-based tools

The current calculators and generators are designed to process ordinary tool inputs in your browser. Do not enter passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal information into a tool.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted using Firebase Hosting. Like most web services, infrastructure may process technical request information such as IP addresses, timestamps, browser details, and requested resources for security, reliability, and operational purposes. Actual retention and processing are governed by the relevant service configuration and provider policies.

Cookies and analytics

The current site does not intentionally require a login or first-party tracking cookie for the tools. If analytics, advertising, or other tracking technologies are introduced, this page should be updated to describe them and any required choices or consent mechanisms.

Third-party links

Toolivo may link to external websites. Their privacy practices are controlled by those websites, not by Toolivo.

Updates

This page may change as the site adds services or features. The effective privacy information should always match the services actually in use.
