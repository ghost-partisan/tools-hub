How to Choose a Domain Name

Generated ideas are only the beginning. A useful domain should also work as a real brand.

Keep it easy to say and spell

Prefer names that are easy to pronounce, type, and remember. Avoid unnecessary punctuation, confusing letter combinations, and spelling that requires explanation.

Check the brand context

Look beyond the domain itself. Consider whether the name fits the product, audience, future expansion, and social or search context of the business.

Verify availability and conflicts

A generated name does not prove that a domain is available. Check registration status directly and consider relevant trademarks or other existing brands before committing to a name.
