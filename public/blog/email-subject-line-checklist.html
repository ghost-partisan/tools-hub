Email Subject Line Checklist

Use a few simple checks before sending an email campaign.

Make the main idea clear

The recipient should have a reasonable idea of what the message contains without needing to decode vague wording.

Review length and punctuation

Very long subjects can be truncated on some screens. Multiple exclamation marks, repeated punctuation, or excessive capitalization can also make a subject feel less natural.

Match the audience and email

Avoid promising something the email does not deliver. Relevance and accurate expectations matter more than trying to trigger a particular scoring formula.

Do not confuse a checklist with deliverability prediction

No subject-line heuristic can guarantee inbox placement. Sender reputation, authentication, message content, engagement, recipient systems, and provider policies all matter.
