About Tools Hub

Tools Hub is a collection of simple browser-based utilities for ecommerce, domains, email marketing, and online business.

Our approach

The goal is straightforward: make useful tools that load quickly, require no account for basic use, and explain the assumptions behind their results.

Estimates are not guarantees

Calculators use the values you enter and may simplify real-world costs. Generated names are ideas, not availability checks. Email analysis is a heuristic editing aid, not a prediction of inbox placement or spam filtering.

Questions or corrections

If you find an error or outdated assumption, please review the site's current information before relying on a result for a financial or business decision.
