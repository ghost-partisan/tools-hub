How the calculation works

Revenue is your selling price. Percentage-based costs include the referral fee, advertising, and returns reserve. Fixed per-unit costs include product cost, FBA fulfillment, inbound shipping, and storage/other costs. Net profit is revenue minus all of those costs.

The break-even selling price estimates the price at which profit reaches zero while keeping your percentage-based costs unchanged.
