What this subject line checker can and cannot tell you

The analyzer can flag common writing and formatting patterns that may deserve review. It cannot predict whether an email will reach the inbox or be classified as spam.

Deliverability depends on more than the subject line

Sender reputation, authentication such as SPF, DKIM, and DMARC, message content, recipient engagement, sending practices, and mailbox-provider policies all affect deliverability. Treat this tool as a quick subject-line editing aid, not a spam score or inbox-placement guarantee.
